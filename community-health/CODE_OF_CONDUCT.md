# Code of Conduct

Every BetaNYC space, online and physical, including every repository in this organization, operates under the **BetaNYC Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy**.

**Read the full policy:** https://www.beta.nyc/code-of-conduct-anti-harassment-policy/

We are dedicated to a respectful and welcoming environment where all participants are free to express their ideas and identities, provided those expressions do not involve discrimination, harassment, or hate speech. We acknowledge that there is no stagnant "safe space," but rather "safer spaces" that require ongoing effort to maintain.

In these repositories, that means:

- Presume the value of others. Cherish everyone's ideas, skills, and contributions.
- Listen as much as you speak. Ask respectful questions and help demystify context for newcomers.
- Build ideas, tools, and platforms that are open and free for reuse. Prioritize public use, not private gain.
- Prioritize access for people who are traditionally excluded from the civic process.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness a violation, in an issue, a pull request, a comment, or any BetaNYC space, contact our Safer Spaces Team at **saferspaces@beta.nyc**. You may also reach any of the ombudspeople listed in the Anti-Harassment Policy. BetaNYC reserves the right to ask anyone violating these policies not to participate in its events or network of activities.
